01 / Profile

About

Nurudeen Segun Aribisala holds an M.A. in English (Literature) from the University of Lagos and a B.A. in English (Literature) from Lagos State University, where he graduated as the best student in the Literature emphasis.

His career moves fluidly between three worlds. In the classroom, he has lectured in Literature and Communication, built curricula, supervised student projects and mentored undergraduates. In academic administration, he has run a college registry as Acting Registrar, serving as Secretary to the Governing Council and its statutory committees, and supported curriculum design for the MasterCard Foundation's Young Africa Works Programme at KAP Film and Television Academy.

In publishing and the arts, he has edited manuscripts, managed bookstores and marketing campaigns for new titles, directed a literary festival programme, and most recently led cultural policy advocacy at Unchained Vibes Africa, working with artists, practitioners and civil-society leaders on campaigns for artistic freedom and social change.
